Catalog sources

Nebula Bridge keeps four libraries: Movies, Shows, Trakt Next Episodes, and Search Results. Every enabled source below feeds Movies or Shows and is merged with the other sources of its type, so a title that appears in several feeds shows up once. Tick “Own folder” only for a personal list (watchlist, collection, history…) that should get a library of its own. “Home row” is a separate client preference exposed for Astra.
Schedule: Next Episodes at 1 AM and 1 PM; Trending and Box Office daily; Popular, Anticipated, and curated lists weekly. Times use the Jellyfin server’s local clock.

Debrid providers
